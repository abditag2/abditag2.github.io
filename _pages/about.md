I am a Principal Scientist at Amazon AGI Labs, where I work on **model customization for frontier models**: continued pre-training (CPT) with relational datasets, and continual learning methods that let models acquire new knowledge and capabilities without catastrophic forgetting.

I've spent over a decade designing, building, and leading production-grade AI/ML systems at scale — merchant risk and LLM agents at [Stripe](https://stripe.com), conversion modeling at [Pinterest](https://pinterest.com), distributed deep learning on Uber AI's Michelangelo platform (including major contributions to [Horovod](https://github.com/horovod/horovod)), and synthetic data generation at Capital One, where my work produced **110 granted US patents**. I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, where my research on the safety and security of cyber-physical systems won a Qualcomm Innovation Fellowship. The full history is on my [CV](/cv/).

## what I'm exploring

- How enterprises can truly make frontier models their own — continued pre-training on relational and structured data, where most enterprise knowledge actually lives.
- Continual learning as a product primitive: models that keep acquiring new knowledge and capabilities without eroding what they already do well.
- Closing the gap between frontier-model research and production reality, drawing on a decade of shipping ML systems in payments, ads, and infrastructure.
