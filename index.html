Current Focus

Most of the proprietary enterprise data is absent from frontier models’ pretraining corpora. It comes in two shapes: large, highly structured relational sources such as tables and catalogs, and small, niche bodies of internal documentation such as contracts and policies. My current focus is on injecting both types of knowledge into language models while minimizing regression in the skills they already have.
